## Practical color utilities for design and development

AllTools Color Tools help designers, developers and content creators translate color formats, build repeatable palettes and evaluate accessible contrast. Every calculation runs locally in the browser without uploading color choices or project data.

## Formats and accessibility

The collection supports HEX and alpha, RGB, HSL, HSV and CMYK where relevant. Contrast tools use the WCAG relative-luminance formula and report AA and AAA outcomes for normal and large text.
