## Build the color workflow you need

Use **conversion tools** when you already have a color and need another notation or model, such as HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV or CMYK. Use **palette and generation tools** to explore related hues, tints, shades, mixtures or gradients. For readable interfaces, use the **contrast and accessibility tools** to compare foreground and background colors against WCAG thresholds.

## Formats and accessibility

Color-model conversions can involve rounding and CMYK results depend on the mathematical conversion used rather than a printer profile. Contrast tools use the WCAG relative-luminance formula and report AA and AAA outcomes for normal and large text. Calculations and previews run locally.
