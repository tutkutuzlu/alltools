## Compare energy across domains

The joule is the reference. The catalog defines `1 cal = 4.184 J`, `1 kcal = 4,184 J`, `1 Wh = 3,600 J`, `1 kWh = 3.6 MJ`, and uses the International Table BTU value. Foot-pounds are also available for mechanical work.

A household use of `2 kWh` converts to `7.2 MJ`. A food label value of `500 kcal` converts to `2.092 MJ`; here “Calorie” in nutrition commonly means kilocalorie, so selecting the correct catalog unit matters.

Energy is accumulated capacity or work, not the rate at which it is used. A 2 kW appliance running for one hour consumes 2 kWh, but this converter does not multiply power by time. Heating values and food energy can also depend on measurement conventions beyond the unit relationship.

Use [Power Converter](../../power-converter/) for watts and horsepower, or [Torque Converter](../../torque-converter/) for rotational moment. Although a newton-meter and joule share dimensions, torque and energy describe different physical concepts.
