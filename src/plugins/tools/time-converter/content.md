## Convert fixed durations

Seconds are the reference. Milliseconds, minutes, hours, 24-hour days and seven-day weeks use fixed factors. The year option is explicitly `365.2425 days`, or `31,556,952 seconds`, representing the average Gregorian calendar year.

A duration of `90 min` becomes `5,400 s` or `1.5 h`. Converting `1 year` to days returns `365.2425`, which is an average rather than the length of a specific calendar year.

This is not a clock, timezone or date converter. It does not handle daylight-saving transitions, leap seconds, business days, months or the actual number of days between two dates. Even “one day” is treated as exactly 86,400 seconds regardless of local clock changes.
