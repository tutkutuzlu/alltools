## Count your text instantly

Paste or type text into the editor to see its word count, character count, sentence count and estimated reading time. Results update as you type.

## How to use the Word Counter

1. Type or paste your text into the input area.
2. Review the live statistics below the editor.
3. Use Clear when you want to start again.

## What does it count?

- **Words:** groups of letters or numbers, including contractions.
- **Characters:** every character, with and without spaces.
- **Sentences:** text segments ending with common sentence punctuation.
- **Reading time:** an estimate based on 200 words per minute.

## Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser. This tool does not send the content you enter to a server.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the counter include punctuation?

Punctuation contributes to the character count but is not counted as a separate word.

### Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. The editor and result cards are designed for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
