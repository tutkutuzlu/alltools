## Explore neighboring hues

Analogous schemes group colors from the same region of the hue wheel. This generator converts the base to HSL and creates five positions at −60, −30, 0, +30 and +60 degrees. It preserves the base saturation and lightness, then returns opaque HEX swatches.

Starting with a blue anchor gives a sequence that moves through nearby violet, blue and cyan regions. Such a family can help establish illustration layers, subtle category identities or a gradient direction without jumping to the opposite side of the wheel.

The five positions are fixed, so the result is reproducible rather than adaptive. A 120-degree total span is broader than some traditional three-color analogous schemes. HSL spacing also does not guarantee equal perceptual distance, and low-saturation input may make the swatches hard to distinguish.

Choose this tool for related color variety. For stronger separation among three anchors, try [Triadic Color Generator](../../triadic-color-generator/). For light and dark states of one hue rather than neighboring hues, use [Tint and Shade Generator](../../tint-shade-generator/).
