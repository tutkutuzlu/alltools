## Compare distance and dimension units

Meters are the reference. The inch is exactly `0.0254 m`, which defines feet, yards and the international mile; the nautical mile is exactly `1,852 m`. Metric units scale by decimal powers.

A road distance of `100 mi` converts to `160.9344 km`, while `10 nmi` becomes `18.52 km`. The nautical option is useful for marine and aviation distance, where it must not be confused with the shorter statute mile.

These unit relationships are defined, but the measured input may not be exact. The converter handles scalar length only: it does not calculate route distance, account for map projection or convert feet-and-inches notation entered as mixed text. Enter one finite numeric value in the selected source unit.
