## Frequently asked questions

### Does decoding verify the signature?

No. Decoding only reveals readable claims. Trust a token only after server-side signature verification.

### Can I use this tool on mobile?

Yes. The editor, controls and results adapt to narrow screens and remain available from a keyboard.
