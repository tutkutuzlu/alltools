## Deterministic placeholder text by unit

Choose words, sentences or paragraphs and an amount. Word mode is clamped to 1–500. Sentence and paragraph modes are clamped to 1–50. Each generated sentence contains ten words in sentence mode; each paragraph contains four twelve-word sentences.

The generator cycles through a fixed 31-word list with deterministic offsets. Requesting the same unit and amount always produces the same output—there is no random vocabulary. Three paragraphs can quickly fill a card layout or test vertical rhythm without writing final copy.

Placeholder text does not represent realistic language length, accessibility needs, localization expansion or actual content structure. It should never replace meaningful labels, error messages or published prose, and deterministic repetition becomes visible in large samples.
