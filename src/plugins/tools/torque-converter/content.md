## Convert rotational moment

Newton-meters are the reference. The catalog includes kN·m, kgf·m, lb·ft and lb·in. One pound-foot is approximately `1.3558179483314 N·m`, and one kilogram-force meter uses standard gravity at `9.80665 N·m`.

An engine specification of `300 N·m` converts to about `221.269 lb·ft`. This is useful when comparing automotive or tool specifications published in different unit systems. Pound-inch is separate from pound-foot: twelve lb·in equal one lb·ft.

Torque is force acting through a perpendicular lever arm. Although a newton-meter has the same base dimensions as a joule, torque is not automatically energy; work also depends on angular displacement. The converter does not calculate power, rotational speed, bolt preload or losses.

Use [Force Converter](../../force-converter/) for linear force and [Power Converter](../../power-converter/) for energy transfer rate. Those quantities require additional physical information to derive from torque.
